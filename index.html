ASC

I work on an outsourced accounting team, so in a given week I might be closing books for a biotech, a construction company and a couple others. Something always comes up that I haven't dealt with in a while, a lease gets modified or a client signs a weird revenue contract, and I end up with fifteen tabs of the Codification open looking for the one paragraph that matters.

Codified is my fix for that. You ask it the way you'd ask a coworker and it points you to the right topic and explains it in normal words. You should still check the cite before it goes in a workpaper, but it gets you to the right page a lot faster than searching on your own.
