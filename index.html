Ask GAAP a question in plain English.

Codified is a chatbot trained on US GAAP sources. Type what you'd ask the senior on your team and it tells you which ASC topic applies and what it actually says. The chat is in the bottom right corner if you want to try it.

01 / why

Why I'm building this

I work on an outsourced accounting team, so in a given week I might be closing books for a biotech, a construction company and a couple others. Something always comes up that I haven't dealt with in a while, a lease gets modified or a client signs a weird revenue contract, and I end up with fifteen tabs of the Codification open looking for the one paragraph that matters.

Codified is my fix for that. You ask it the way you'd ask a coworker and it points you to the right topic and explains it in normal words. You should still check the cite before it goes in a workpaper, but it gets you to the right page a lot faster than searching on your own.

02 / examples

Stuff people actually ask it

  • ASC 606Customer can return product within 30 days. Do I book all the revenue now?
  • ASC 842Landlord gave us 3 free months up front. How does that hit the lease liability?
  • 350-40Can we capitalize the implementation fees on our new cloud ERP?
  • ASC 450Client is getting sued. Do we accrue something or just disclose it?
  • ASC 718Employee quit before their options vested. What happens to the expense already booked?
