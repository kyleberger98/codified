The bot runs on Chatling and was trained on a set of public US GAAP websites I picked out. When you ask something it pulls from those pages, names the ASC topic it thinks applies and walks through it. If the question needs an entry, it'll usually give you one.

01

The problem

The Codification is organized by topic number, which is great if you already know the topic number. Most of the time you don't. You know a client did something odd and you need to figure out what part of GAAP even covers it, and Google mostly gives you Big 4 PDFs from 2015 or blog posts trying to sell you software.

On a small outsourced team there usually isn't a technical accounting person to walk over and ask. So you dig, and the digging comes out of the time you were supposed to spend on recs and the reporting package.

02

A real example

This is roughly what a back and forth looks like. Numbers are made up.

We sold 100 units at $100 each. Customers can return them within 30 days and historically about 10% come back. Cost is $60 a unit. What do I book?

This is a sale with a right of return, which ASC 606 treats as variable consideration. 606-10-55-22 to 55-29

You only recognize revenue for the units you don't expect to come back, so 90 units, or $9,000. The other $1,000 goes to a refund liability. On the cost side, set up an asset for the inventory you expect to get back instead of expensing all of it.

04

What it won't do

It isn't a CPA and it doesn't know your client. It can be wrong, especially on newer ASUs or anything that turns on a judgment call. Treat it like a smart first-year who read the standard last night: good for getting pointed in the right direction, and you still review it before anything gets signed off.

It also only covers US GAAP right now. No IFRS, no tax code.

05

Where it's headed

For now it's free and it lives in the chat window on this site. If it keeps being useful, the plan is a paid version for small firms where each client gets its own folder of past questions, so the next person who picks up that client can see what was already researched and why.
