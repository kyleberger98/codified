How it works

From "where is that in the ASC?" to an answer you can use

Most technical accounting questions don't need a Big 4 memo. They need someone who knows where to look. Codified is trained on authoritative US GAAP guidance and explains it the way a good senior would at your desk.

The problem we're solving

The FASB Accounting Standards Codification has roughly 90 topics and thousands of paragraphs. Finding the right one is slow, and reading it is slower.

1

Small teams, big questions

Outsourced accounting firms and lean finance teams handle complex clients without a technical accounting department to lean on.

2

Research eats the close

An hour spent searching for the right subtopic on a lease modification is an hour not spent on reconciliations and reporting.

3

Search isn't built for GAAP

Generic search returns blog posts and outdated pre-codification guidance. You need the current standard and a plain explanation.

How Codified works

Three steps, usually under ten seconds.

Ask in plain English

Type the question the way you'd ask a coworker, like "Our customer can return product within 30 days. How do we handle that under 606?" No need to know the topic number.

Codified finds the guidance

The assistant is trained on curated sources covering the ASC topics, so it pulls the relevant section instead of guessing from the open internet.

Get an answer with a reference

You get a short explanation, the ASC topic to cite, and when it matters, an example journal entry. Paste it into your workpaper notes and keep moving.

FAQ

Does Codified replace a CPA or technical accounting review?

No. It's a research starting point. For material judgments, audit positions, or anything going in front of a client, have a qualified professional review the conclusion.

Does it cover IFRS?

Not yet. Codified focuses on US GAAP under the FASB Codification. IFRS comparisons are on the roadmap.
